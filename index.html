Start thinking

You do not need a finished character. Come with a feeling. These are the three questions I will ask you in character on the night, plus one more. You can pass on any of them.

  1. A door your character will not walk through. Some choices are impossible for them, whatever the cost.
  2. What you want more of. Sneaking, fighting, scheming, talking your way past a guard, or something else.
  3. One thing they care about. A person, a place, a promise, a hope. Small is fine.
  4. What their hands do. Fight, sneak, watch, or talk.

You burn one metal, and you start only partly awake to it. Opening the Dossier and running its questions once is the best preparation there is.

Bring

  • Something to write with, and a laptop or tablet for the Dossier. A phone works too.
  • A snack. Wenna says an empty stomach makes for poor decisions.
  • Any line, veil or worry you would like me to know about. You can tell me privately at any time.
Keep your copy safeThe Dossier saves to your own browser only. When you finish, use its export button and send me the file, so nothing is lost if your browser clears itself.
